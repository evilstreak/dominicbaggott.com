Specimens of work & opinion

These are notes from the middle of something. I’ve been building for the web my entire adult life, and the tools I use every day have changed more in the last two years than in all the years before them. So I’m writing down what I’ve built and what I think while it’s happening, and typesetting each piece in a way that would have been completely impractical for me before whatever this is.

The businesses that get to grips with this now will do brilliantly. The ones waiting to see how it shakes out are going to find out the hard way.

I’m Dominic Baggott. I help leadership teams work out what this means for them – and then get on with it. If you’re thinking of working with me, click around and read a few of these first: they show how I think, so you’ll know what you’d be getting yourself into.
